Building on last year's mission, we focused on helping people join FIRST and fostering meaningful connections through outreach events, which remained a core part of our team's efforts throughout the season.

 

We held events at 6 libraries, including Shoreline, Broadview, Lynnwood, Puyallup, Greenlake, and Edmonds branches. Additionally, we partnered with Young Black & Brilliant for one of their library book clubs. During these outreach events, we typically give presentations, teach kids simple block coding, and allow them to drive both FLL and FTC-style robots. Additionally, we hosted numerous booths at large events such as Seattle Worldcon and launched our Coding Class at the Bitterlake Community Center.
