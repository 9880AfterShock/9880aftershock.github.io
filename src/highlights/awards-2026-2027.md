This season was one of the most successful in our team's history. We earned a total of 4 awards.


Our greatest accomplishment was earning the 2nd-place Sustain Award at the Michiana Premier Event, an international-level competition in South Bend, Indiana. Competing against 96 FIRST Tech Challenge teams from around the world, our team was recognized for its long-term success both on and off the field.


Throughout the season, we participated in league meets, hosted a scrimmage with four other FTC teams, and competed at the Interleague tournament, where we earned the 1st-place Winning Alliance Award and the 2nd-place Inspire Award. These achievements advanced us to the Super Qualifier, where we qualified for the Washington State Championship and were the 1st-place seed.


At the Washington State Championship, we earned the 1st-place Sustain Award and qualified for the Michiana Premier Event, where we concluded our season.
