This season, our team organized 19 outreach events, totaling 110 hours of community engagement. We hosted presentations, STEM booths, workshops, and demonstrations at a variety of locations throughout our community.

During these events, participants had the opportunity to drive FIRST robots, explore engineering concepts through hands-on activities, and learn more about robotics and STEM careers.

​

Our outreach included STEM workshops at local libraries, presentations at schools, educational workshops for students, and interactive FIRST and STEM booths at large community events such as the Washington State Fair.
