By helping younger students develop confidence, technical skills, and a passion for STEM, we are building a stronger and more connected FIRST community for years to come.

​

We mentored 10 FIRST LEGO League teams in the Seattle–Edmonds area, providing technical guidance, answering questions, and encouraging younger students throughout their seasons. We aim to not only mentor teams but also to create lasting support for FIRST programs through fundraising and community partnerships.
